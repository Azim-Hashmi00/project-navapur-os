# Project Navapur

## Project Summary

Project Navapur is a hyperlocal commerce and local services startup focused on digitizing Navapur. The goal is to become the city's trusted digital platform where residents can discover, buy, book, and access local businesses, products, and services from one place.

## Project Type
Hyperlocal Commerce & Local Services Startup

## Working Name
Project Navapur

## Mission
Build the operating system for Navapur.

## Vision
Create one trusted digital platform where every resident of Navapur can discover, buy, book, and access everything available locally.

## Problem

Residents of Navapur use platforms like Amazon and Flipkart for online shopping, but local businesses, shops, and service providers have little or no digital presence.

Customers cannot easily:

- Discover local businesses
- Order from nearby shops
- Find local services
- Compare local options
- Receive fast local delivery

Meanwhile, local businesses struggle to reach customers online.

Project Navapur aims to bridge this gap.

## Long-Term Goal

Become the primary digital platform for Navapur.

Eventually include:

- Shopping
- Food Delivery
- Grocery
- Medicines
- Clothing
- Electronics
- Hardware
- Local Businesses
- Electricians
- Plumbers
- Mechanics
- Clinics
- Beauty Services
- Home Services
- Hotels
- Events
- Local Deals
- Business Tools
- AI Assistants
- Digital Payments

## Current Stage

Phase 0 — Market Research

Nothing has been built yet.

The first goal is validating whether this startup should exist.

## Startup Principles

- Solve real problems.
- Validate before building.
- Customers before features.
- Business before technology.
- Start simple.
- Think long term.
- Build trust with local businesses.
- Scale only after validation.

## First Success Goal

Launch a simple MVP with a small number of local businesses and acquire the first real customers before expanding.
